Recurring inspections

Calendar and hour-based items. Due dates compute from the date or time complied. Verify each against the aircraft's operating limitations — an experimental in day VFR carries a shorter list than an IFR machine.

AD & SB compliance

Applicability first. ADs are issued against type-certificated products. On an amateur-built airframe the airframe ADs generally don't apply, but ADs against the installed engine, propeller, magnetos, and appliances normally do. Check each against the FAA AD database by make/model/serial and record how you determined applicability.

About this tool

The checklist merges 14 CFR part 43 appendix D with items specific to RV-derived taildraggers and the Harmon Rocket. It is a documentation aid, not an approved data source. You are the inspector of record — the maintenance manual, the operating limitations, the manufacturers' service information, and your judgment govern.
